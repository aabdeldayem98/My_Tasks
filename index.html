My Tasks

Developed by Ahmed Abdel-Dayem · LinkedIn

A simple, focused space to organize your tasks, manage your priorities, and keep everything that matters in one place.

What’s inside?

Focus Hub

Turn a long to-do list into clear actions — Priority, Schedule, Assign, or Remove.

Calendar

Keep deadlines, schedules, and upcoming tasks visible so nothing important slips through.

Gantt Chart

Plan projects visually, track timelines, understand dependencies, and see what’s coming next.

Project Data

Keep your project information, progress, tasks, timelines, and key details organized in one place.

Smart Categories

Organize your life into Growth, Finance, and Personal — because not every task belongs in the same bucket.

Task Management

Create tasks, set priorities, add deadlines and reminders, track progress, and keep your notes attached to the task.

Dashboard

Get a quick snapshot of what needs your attention today, what’s coming next, and how much you’ve already accomplished.

Built for clarity. Built for focus. Built to keep things moving.
